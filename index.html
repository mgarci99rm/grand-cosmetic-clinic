01 — The team

Two clinicians, one philosophy: enhance, don't erase.

Every treatment plan is built around your own anatomy and pace — no default protocols, no rushed rooms.

Dr. Behrang Dehkordi

MD — Sports Medicine & Non-Surgical Orthopedics

Board-certified in sports medicine, Dr. Dehkordi trained at the American University of the Caribbean and completed his residency at Hennepin County Medical Center, followed by a fellowship at the University of Illinois at Chicago. A former college soccer player, he has worked as team physician for university and professional soccer clubs, and brings that same attention to musculoskeletal detail into the clinic's aesthetic work.

Tricia

Nurse Practitioner — Aesthetic Medicine

Raised between New York and the Philippines, Tricia earned her nursing degree in 2014 and her Master's in 2021. She leads the clinic's injectable and laser work — Botox, Dysport and Jeuveau, dermal fillers, and medical weight-loss support — with a focus on results that read as rested, not redone.
