01 — Our Beauty Treatments

Know what you're getting, before you see the price.

A quick guide to each treatment — what it is, and what it can help with.

Botox

A neuromodulator that relaxes the small muscles behind expression lines.

Softens forehead lines, crow's feet & frown lines

Dysport

Another FDA-approved neuromodulator, often chosen for a faster onset over larger areas.

Great for the forehead & broader treatment zones

Jeuveau

The newest neuromodulator option, formulated specifically for frown lines.

Clean, predictable results between the brows

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid gel used to restore volume and soften definition.

Lips, cheeks, jawline, under-eyes, hands & more

Hylenex

An enzyme that safely dissolves misplaced or excess filler.

Corrects or adjusts previous filler work

Kybella

An injectable that permanently breaks down stubborn fat beneath the chin.

A more defined jawline, without surgery

Grand Cosmetic Clinic was built around a simple idea: aesthetic medicine should feel like a conversation with someone who knows your face, not a menu of add-ons. What started as a small injectables practice in Studio City has grown into a physician-led med spa trusted across the Valley — without losing the unhurried, one-on-one feel it started with.

Every treatment plan here is built around your own anatomy and pace. No default protocols, no rushed rooms — just two clinicians who enhance, rather than erase.
